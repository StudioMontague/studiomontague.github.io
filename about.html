The Solodev

Graham Ainge

Founder & Lead Developer

Graham is the founder — and, for now, the only member — of Studio Montague. Before games, he spent over a decade teaching science, and before that he toured as a keyboardist and vocalist in a rock band, signed to Island Records.

Playing narrative-driven games with three older siblings since the Amiga 500 era, titles like Monkey Island (aged 7), Ocarina of Time (aged 14), and Skyrim (too old), shaped how he thinks about worlds and stories. The gameplay of other titles, such as Turrican, Goldeneye and Halo were equally capturing for their competitive edge and precision.

Seed.Runner draws on all three chapters — the discipline of teaching, the collaboration of a band, and a lifelong love of games built to be explored.

He works out of a home studio in Brighton in the UK, with two whippets asleep in the corner.
